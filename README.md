## 💫 What is PoseKit?

PoseKit takes the fiddly parts out of posing and animations. It finds the animation mods you already have, lets you nudge your character into exactly the right spot, and remembers it all so you only have to set up once.

Great for screenshots, RP, and anyone who has ever spent ten minutes lining up a hug. 🤗

---

## 🌸 Features

### 🎭 Animation Browser
PoseKit finds the animation and pose mods in your Penumbra library for you. Browse them in one place and hit **Play**. No digging through mod settings.

### 🎯 Live Offset
Drag your character into position while the animation plays: closer, further, higher, turned just right.

### 📚 Presets
Save any pose, including its position, as a named preset and replay it with one click. Organize large libraries with groups, search and filters.

### 📍 Anchors
Presets can remember *where* they happened: a spot in the world, a piece of furniture, or your partner. When you play them again, you land right back in place.

### 💞 Pairing
Pair up with a friend to play together:
- Send a pairing invite and play **couple presets**, both halves at once
- Queue up poses so you're both ready at the same time
- Your partner always gets to accept or decline

### 🦴 Bone Align
Characters come in all shapes and sizes, and couple animations don't always line up. Pick a body part on you and one on your partner, press **Align**, and PoseKit closes the gap for you.

### 🧠 Alignment Memory
Once you've aligned an animation, PoseKit remembers it. Next time you play it together, it lines up on its own.

### 🔄 Emote Sync
Restart everyone's emotes nearby at the same moment, so a group dance really stays in sync on your screen.

### 🎥 Free Camera
Unlock the camera and fly around freely for the perfect angle. Your pose stays exactly where it is.

### 👠 SimpleHeels Friendly
Using SimpleHeels? PoseKit can hand its offsets to it too.

## 📦 Installation

1. In game, type `/xlsettings` and open the **Experimental** tab
2. Under **Custom Plugin Repositories**, paste:
   ```
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RaylaPetal/PoseKit/master/repo.json
   ```
3. Tick the box, click **Save**
4. Open `/xlplugins`, search for **PoseKit**, and install

**You'll also need:** [Penumbra](https://github.com/xivdev/Penumbra) with some animation mods installed.
**Optional:** SimpleHeels, for sharing offsets with it.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Type `/posekit` to open the window
2. Choose which mod folders to scan in **Settings**
3. Open the **Animations** tab and press **Play** on any pose
4. Use **Live Offset** to get into position
5. Save it under **Presets** to play it again later ✨

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/posekit` | Open or close PoseKit |
| `/posekit tfc` | Turn free camera on or off |
| `/posekit sync` | Resync nearby emotes |
